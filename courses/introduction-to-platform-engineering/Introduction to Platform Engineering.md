# Introduction to Platform Engineering

### Welcome to the course
Ready to take your career to the next level? This introductory course to platform engineering will give you the essential knowledge to understand this rapidly growing field and embark on your platform engineering journey. Discover why platform engineering matters and how it's taking over the industry, especially in how enterprise software is built and delivered.

This course consists of five video lessons:

1. Why platform engineering matters
2. From DevOps to platform engineering
3. Key definitions
4. Why platform engineering is a multiplayer game
5. How to get started with your platform engineering journey

Once you took the video lessons, please take the quiz to get your course completion badge.
